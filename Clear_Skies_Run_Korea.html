Clear Skies Run

Steer your field agent across South Korea to reach AirKorea monitoring stations. Pollution is invisible until the GEMS satellite scans over it, and westerly winds keep pushing haze across the Yellow Sea. Keep your lungs clean until 18:00 KST.

Mission: calibrate the AirKorea network

  • Tap, click or drag to send your agent somewhere. Arrow keys and WASD work too.
  • Reach the flashing stations for 100 points each. You stay south of the DMZ.
  • GEMS sweeps east to west from its orbit over Korea. Colors show what it saw, then fade.
  • When the beam passes you, it pings a surface estimate with its error bar.
  • Haze plumes ride the westerlies in over the Yellow Sea. Watch the wind.
  • Ozone builds through the afternoon, downwind of Seoul, Ulsan and Busan.
