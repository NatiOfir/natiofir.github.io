Clear Skies Run

Steer your field agent across North America to reach monitoring stations. Pollution is invisible until the TEMPO satellite scans over it, so read its signals and keep your lungs clean until 18:00.

Mission: calibrate the ground network

  • Tap, click or drag to send your agent somewhere. Arrow keys and WASD work too.
  • Reach the flashing stations for 100 points each.
  • TEMPO sweeps east to west. Colors show what it saw, then fade.
  • When the beam passes you, it pings a TempoNet estimate with its error bar.
  • Ozone builds through the afternoon, downwind of cities.
